## Update procedure

1. Confirm the current beach opening period and lifeguard hours.
2. Confirm parking capacity, operating hours and price.
3. Confirm shower, locker, changing-room and pet rules.
4. Confirm fireworks and market dates.
5. Check that each restaurant is still operating and that its name has not changed.
6. Update `src/data/site.json`, FAQ copy, `StatusBar.astro`, and the page metadata.
